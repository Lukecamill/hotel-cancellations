# Brief to the simulated revenue manager

Prepared 15 November 2026 for the review of ADR 0005. Reviewer: Revenue manager, simulated with OpenAI Codex. AI use is declared. This is a coursework decision handoff, not real hotel authority.

## Role and request

Act as the revenue manager for City Hotel and Resort Hotel. Challenge the assumptions and the evidence before deciding whether to accept, accept with changes or reject the recommendation. State the selected operating point and reasons. Treat all quantities and costs as historical coursework proxies. Do not infer verified capacity, real guest parties, actual cash losses or authority to send messages or relocate guests.

## Context and assumptions

The supplied fixed booking learner is unchanged. The Stage 5 policy study selects using October–December 2016 validation only, 92 arrival nights per hotel. The candidate calibration uses outcomes mature at the decision date. The frozen 2017 holdout was not used to select these Stage 5 policies, and the new candidates have not received a fresh holdout evaluation.

Reference business assumptions: decision seven days before arrival, walking cost EUR250 per excess booking-room, variable cost EUR20 per occupied room-night, zero resale. Margin uses nightly median ADR less variable cost, floored at zero. Nights are assumed sold out; true capacity and multi-night inventory are unavailable. Group blocks are exact-key matches, not verified travel parties. Economic settings are scenario assumptions, not estimates from operating records.

## Options and recommendation

1. No additional overbooking, q=0. Reference-scenario validation cost proxies: EUR423.43/night City and EUR42.21/night Resort.
2. Stage 5 City fixed mature calibration-window rate: EUR331.41/night, selected saving EUR92.01/night, 0.25 walked booking-rooms/night, mean q approximately 2.109.
3. Stage 5 Resort model plus blocks and 12-week re-level: EUR38.92/night, selected saving EUR3.29/night, zero observed walked booking-rooms across the 92 validation nights, mean q approximately 0.022.
4. Alternative 4/8/12-week, independent-booking, horizon and resale scenarios are in the complete grid. Do not choose cost/resale assumptions to maximise an apparent saving. The original C2 Resort 8-week reference is a distinct preserved baseline, not this Stage 5 proposal.

The project recommendation in ADR 0005 nominates options 2 and 3 for further review, retaining q=0 pending agreement on operating constraints. Savings use the same validation sample that selected the winners, so they are optimistic descriptions rather than independent expected profits. The initial seven-day model calibration ratios are about 0.46 City and 0.76 Resort; the separate passing booking-horizon calibrator does not repair this horizon.

Please decide both whether the historical analysis may be retained and whether positive additional quantities should be accepted as the operating point. Give a clear fallback, prerequisites for reconsideration and the handoff owner.
