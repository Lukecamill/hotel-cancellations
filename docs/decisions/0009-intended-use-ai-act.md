# ADR 0009 — intended use and AI Act assessment boundary

Date: 8 November 2026. Status: working coursework assessment; reassess before operational deployment.

The present system is a local historical-data demonstration of cancellation prediction, aggregate nightly overbooking advice, simulated manager review and monitoring. It does not choose real guests to relocate or update live room availability.

Assess this testing activity separately from a hotel deployment. Article 2(8) is the relevant conditional pre-deployment activity exclusion; there is no blanket student exemption. For a potential ordinary commercial hotel use with the stated purpose, no Article 6/Annex I or Annex III high-risk route has been identified: record an other/non-high-risk working classification rather than claiming that human approval itself guarantees low risk. No prohibited Article 5 practice has been identified in the present functionality.

Provider status depends on who develops/has developed and supplies or puts the system into service under its name; deployer status depends on use under an entity's authority. Do not assign a real hotel role to the local simulation. The hypothetical supplying entity would be provider, and the operating hotel deployer; in-house hotel development can combine both roles.

The applicable reference is the AI Act as amended in July 2026. Reassess scope, role allocation, transparency and other law before use changes, including individual guest treatment, public-benefit eligibility or a future generative interface. Classification does not establish predictive reliability or business approval.

See [the classification research note](../diary1-ai-act-classification.md) for the cited legal provisions, current application dates, source register and limitations. This ADR records project intent and a research conclusion, not regulatory clearance.
